Needlbar v0.3.8 gives Settings cards consistent room at their edges.

- The shared Settings card shell adds 24pt horizontal and 20pt vertical padding across provider cards, system information cards, and general Settings cards, including Layout, Notifications, and Data.
- Claude, Codex, Cursor, CPU, RAM, Disk, and Network card content uses the shared outer spacing without duplicating child gutters.
- Internal row spacing, control sizing, captions, and spacing around separators remain in place.

Authentication, quota retrieval and refresh, usage collectors, and refresh timers are unchanged. This release adds no provider, credential, or authentication flow.
